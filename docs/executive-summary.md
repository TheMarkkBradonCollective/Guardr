## Problem & Solution

**Problem:** Hiring licensed security in California is slow, opaque, and agency-centric. Guards lack a direct channel to find work; clients wait days for quotes with little visibility into who is on site.

**Solution:** Guardr is a map-first marketplace where guards browse and accept jobs directly and clients approve who works their site. The platform handles credential verification, booking, in-app payments, live shift tracking, and post-shift reporting.

---

## Business Model

Transactional platform fees only — no subscriptions. Fee embedded in client hourly rate at booking.

| Party | Example ($35/hr client rate) |
|-------|------------------------------|
| Client pays | $35/hr |
| Guard receives | $30/hr |
| Platform fee | $5/hr |

**Unit economics (8-hr shift):** ~$40 platform fee → ~$11.58 net to company after Stripe and staff share (~4.1% of GMV).

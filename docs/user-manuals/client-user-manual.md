# Guardr Client User Manual

For businesses, venues, properties, and event organizers who need licensed security coverage.

Guardr is operated by **Signature Security Specialist, LLC** as a technology marketplace only. You hire independent contractor guards through the platform — Guardr is not your security company, employer, or dispatcher.

**Related:** [Quick Start](./quick-start.md) · [Guard Manual](./guard-user-manual.md) · In-app **Guide** (account menu)

---

## 1. Account setup

1. Go to [guardr.co](https://guardr.co) and choose **I need security for my site**.
2. Create your client account and complete company/contact details under **Profile**.
3. On **Home**, watch for **Account pending approval**.
4. While pending you can use **Home**, **Profile**, **Messages**, and **Guide**. Most ops pages stay blocked until staff approve you.
5. After approval, the full client workspace opens.

**Not hiring guards?** If you want a job *at Guardr* (ops/support), use **Apply to work at Guardr** instead — not this client path.

## 2. Main pages

| Page | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Map** | Job geography, pins, live shift tracking |
| **Home** | Status, quick actions, live coverage, reports shortcuts |
| **Jobs** | Post/track jobs, pay, approve guards, confirm audits, overtime, ratings |
| **Guards** | Browse profiles; send a direct request to one guard |

| **Payments** | Invoices, PDF download, pay when due |
| **Profile** / **Settings** / **Guide** | Account details, preferences, this product’s help |

Navigation layout differs slightly on phone, tablet, and desktop; page names stay the same.

## 3. Post a marketplace job

Open work any qualified guard can apply to.

1. **Home → Post job offer**, or **Jobs → + Post offer**.
2. Enter site/title, address, schedule, requirements, instructions, and pay/billing.
3. On location: **Use current location** if you are on site, or enter address/coordinates manually.
4. Review the summary → **Slide to post job offer**.
5. Status starts as **Pending review** until staff approve the listing → then **Open**.

Staff review job offers from **Applications** / **Jobs**. If declined, update the job as instructed and resubmit when allowed.

---

## 4. Request a specific guard

1. Open **Guards** → select a profile.
2. Review resume, licenses, experience, and availability.
3. **Send assignment request to [name]** → complete site, schedule, instructions, and pay.
4. **Slide to send request to [name]**.
5. Track it under **Jobs**.

Unlike marketplace jobs, you already chose the guard — no later **Approve guard** step for that same person. The guard still must **claim** the request, and payment/staff rules still apply.

---

## 5. Pay for the job

1. Open **Jobs** → open the job card.
2. Use **Pay Now** / **Pay for this job** (Stripe checkout).
3. Confirm payment state back on **Jobs**.

Payment may be required before guard assignment, depending on platform settings.

Formal invoices also appear under **Payments** after staff approve billing on completed work (**Draft** → **Payment due** → **Paid**). Use **Download PDF** and **Pay by card** when due.

---

## 6. Approve or decline applicants (marketplace)

1. Open **Jobs** → open an **Open** job.
2. Review the guard who applied.
3. **Approve guard** → job becomes **Accepted**, or **Decline guard** → keep the job open.

Applications go **to you**, not to a staff dispatcher. Guardr does not pick who works your site.

---

## 7. During the shift

The **guard** starts and ends the shift. You watch and confirm.

1. **Jobs**, **Home → Live coverage**, or **Map** for assignment and live tracking.
2. **Messages** or job chat to coordinate.
3. When the guard clocks in with photos, open the job → **Guard self-audit photos** → review selfie, uniform, and shoes → **Confirm self-audit photos**.
4. If the guard skipped photos, the job may show **No Self Audit** until they upload.

### Reports

- **Home → Reports** for incident, activity, and property reports.
- **Support → File a report** for formal complaints or safety concerns.
- **Support → Contact support** for help tickets.

---

## 8. Overtime

Late clock-out can create overtime.

1. Open **Jobs** → open the flagged job.
2. Under **Late clock-out overtime**:
   - **Approve overtime $X** if correct, then pay by card when prompted, or
   - **Dispute charge** if incorrect.

---

## 9. Completion and rating

1. Guard ends the shift → job becomes **Completed**.
2. Review reports, photos, overtime, and payment notices on **Jobs**.
3. Rate the guard when the rating action appears.
4. Guard payouts release through the platform after the normal delay unless a dispute holds them.

---

## 10. Typical marketplace sequence

```
Post job → Staff review → Open → You pay → Guard applies → You approve
→ Guard works shift (you confirm self-audit) → Completed → Invoice / rate
```

Direct-request sequence skips “guard applies / you approve” and uses **claim** instead.

# Guardr Client User Manual

For businesses, venues, properties, and event organizers who need licensed security coverage.

---

## Who you hire on Guardr (read this first)

Guardr is operated by **Signature Security Specialist, LLC** as a **technology marketplace only**.

| What Guardr is | What Guardr is not |
|----------------|-------------------|
| A platform to post jobs and connect with **licensed independent contractor guards** | Your security company, PPO, or staffing agency |
| A payment facilitator and credential review tool | The employer or supervisor of guards on your site |
| A way to approve **who** works your property | A dispatcher that assigns guards without your approval (marketplace jobs) |

**Guards on Guardr are independent contractors.** They do **not** work for Guardr. For each job, you engage the guard **directly** for that assignment. Guardr is not a party to your on-site security relationship.

**Guardr staff** (Support through Founder) are **employees of Signature Security** who operate the platform. They verify accounts and credentials, review job listings, and handle payments pipeline — they do **not** provide guards as employees of Guardr.

## 1. Account setup

1. Go to [guardr.co](https://guardr.co) and choose **I need security for my site**.
2. Create your client account and complete company/contact details under **Profile**.
3. On **Home**, watch for **Account pending approval**.
4. While pending you can use **Home**, **Profile**, **Messages**, and **Guide**. Most ops pages stay blocked until staff approve you.
5. After approval, the full client workspace opens.

**Want a job at Guardr (ops/support)?** Use **Apply to work at Guardr** — not the client path.

## 3. Post a marketplace job

Open work any qualified guard can apply to.

1. **Home → Post job offer**, or **Jobs → + Post offer**.
2. Enter site/title, address, schedule, requirements, instructions, and **hourly rate / billing**.
3. On location: **Use current location** if you are on site, or enter address/coordinates manually.
4. Review the summary → **Slide to post job offer**.
5. Status starts as **Pending review** until staff approve the listing → then **Open**.

Staff review job offers from **Applications** / **Jobs**. If declined, update the job as instructed and resubmit when allowed.

---

## 4. Request a specific guard

1. Open **Guards** → select a profile.
2. Review resume, licenses, experience, and availability.
3. **Send assignment request to [name]** → complete site, schedule, instructions, and pay.
4. **Slide to send request to [name]**.
5. Track it under **Jobs**.

You already chose the guard — no later **Approve guard** step for that person. The guard must **claim** the request; payment and staff rules still apply.

## 5. What you pay — costs, fees, and billing

This section explains **your** charges as a client. You pay the **posted hourly rate** (and approved extras). Guardr retains a **platform fee** from that rate — the guard receives the remainder. You typically see the **client rate** on job cards and checkout, not a separate platform fee line.

### 5.1 Base job cost

**Estimated job total** is generally:

```
Hourly rate × billable hours × number of guards needed
```

| Factor | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Hourly rate** | Set when you post the job (or negotiated on open-contract jobs) |
| **Billable hours** | Scheduled duration; unpaid breaks may reduce billable time |
| **Guards needed** | Multi-guard jobs multiply the base |
| **Crew jobs** | Coordinated crew assignments may include a small per-guard rate adjustment on the client bill |

**Example:** $50/hr × 8 hours × 2 guards = **$800** base estimate before overtime or extras.

### 5.2 Platform fee (how Guardr earns)

Guardr's platform fee is embedded in the marketplace economics:

| Party | Receives |
|-------|----------|
| **Guard (contractor)** | Hourly rate minus platform fee per hour |
| **Guardr (platform)** | Platform fee per hour (default **$5/hr flat** unless settings or negotiation differ) |

You pay the **full hourly rate** you agreed to. The guard's pay is calculated from that rate minus the fee. This is **not** an extra line item on most client checkout screens — it is part of how the marketplace price is split.

### 5.3 When you pay

1. **Before the shift (typical):** Pay by card on the job (**Pay Now** / Stripe checkout) after staff approve the listing and before or as guards are assigned.
2. **After completion:** Formal **invoices** may appear under **Payments** for completed work (**Draft** → **Payment due** → **Paid**). Download PDF and pay by card when due.

Payment status on **Jobs** shows whether the job is unpaid, paid, held (complete), or fully settled after guard payout.

### 5.4 Overtime

If a guard clocks out late, **overtime** may be calculated at your hourly rate (× guards as applicable).

1. Open **Jobs** → flagged job → **Late clock-out overtime**.
2. **Approve overtime $X** and pay by card when prompted, or **Dispute charge** if incorrect.

Guard payout is blocked until overtime is resolved.

### 5.5 Schedule changes and extensions

If you extend a job schedule after posting, an **additional amount** may be required before the new schedule applies. Pay when prompted on the job.

### 5.6 Tips (optional)

After a completed job and review, you may leave an optional tip (minimum $1; common presets $5 / $10 / $20). Tips go to the guard through the platform payment flow.

### 5.7 Open-contract / negotiated pricing

On jobs that support price negotiation, you and the guard may agree on an hourly rate. The agreed rate updates what you pay and what the guard earns. Platform fee rules still apply per the negotiated terms shown before you confirm.

### 5.8 What Guardr does not charge you for separately

Guardr staff do **not** bill you as a staffing agency markup on top of an undisclosed guard wage. You contract **directly** with the guard at the **rate shown on the platform** for that job.

## 6. Approve or decline applicants (marketplace)

1. Open **Jobs** → open an **Open** job.
2. Review the guard who applied.
3. **Approve guard** → job becomes **Accepted**, or **Decline guard** → keep the job open.

Applications go **to you**, not to a staff dispatcher. Guardr does not pick who works your site.

---

## 7. During the shift

The **guard** (independent contractor) starts and ends the shift. You watch and confirm.

1. **Jobs**, **Home → Live coverage**, or **Map** for assignment and live tracking.
2. **Messages** or job chat to coordinate.
3. When the guard clocks in with photos, open the job → **Guard self-audit photos** → review → **Confirm self-audit photos**.
4. If the guard skipped photos, the job may show **No Self Audit** until they upload.

### Reports

- **Home → Reports** for incident, activity, and property reports.
- **Support → File a report** for formal complaints or safety concerns.

---

## 8. Completion and rating

1. Guard ends the shift → job becomes **Completed**.
2. Review reports, photos, overtime, and payment notices on **Jobs**.
3. Rate the guard when prompted; optional tip after review.
4. Guard payouts release through the platform after the normal delay unless a dispute holds them.

---

## 9. Typical marketplace sequence

```
Post job → Staff review → Open → You pay → Guard applies → You approve
→ Guard works shift (you confirm self-audit) → Completed → Invoice / rate
```

Direct-request sequence skips “guard applies / you approve” and uses **claim** instead.

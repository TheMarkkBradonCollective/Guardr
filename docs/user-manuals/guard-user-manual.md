# Guardr Guard User Manual

For licensed, independent security professionals who find work on Guardr.

Guardr is operated by **Signature Security Specialist, LLC** as a technology marketplace. You are an **independent contractor**. You choose jobs; clients approve who works their site. Guardr does not employ you, dispatch you like an agency, or supervise you on site.

**Related:** [Quick Start](./quick-start.md) · [Client Manual](./client-user-manual.md) · In-app **Guide** (account menu, after active)

---

## 1. Choose the right signup

- **I'm a guard** — marketplace contractor work (this manual).
- **Apply to work at Guardr** — platform staff jobs (ops/support). Guardr does **not** hire through guard signup.

---

## 2. Account lifecycle

| Status | What you see | What to do |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| **Pending** | Activation screen — application under review | Upload credentials while staff reviews |
| **Approved** | Activation screen — finish credentials | Keep uploading; staff verify documents |
| **Active** | Full app (Map, Jobs, Pay, Messages, …) | Find work and run shifts |

While pending/approved: **Map**, **Jobs**, **Pay**, **Messages**, **Profile**, and **Guide** stay blocked. Account menu is mainly **Settings** / sign out. Approved guards can use **Contact support** (activation help).

## 3. Credentials required to go live

Staff verify these before marketplace access. Nothing auto-verifies on upload.

| # | Credential | What to upload |
|---|------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Government ID** | Front, back, selfie, state, number, expiration |
| 2 | **Certificate of Insurance (COI)** | General liability COI |
| 3 | **BSIS Guard Card** | Registration card photo |
| 4 | **Mandatory training (PTA / UOF)** | Power to Arrest **and** Appropriate Use of Force as **separate** certificates |
| 5 | **Continuing Education** | Full **32-hour** first-year BSIS package (**9** certificates) |

**32-hour CE package (9 certificates):** Communication (4), Public Relations (4), Observation & Documentation (4), Liability/Legal (4), Officer Safety (4), Trespass (4), Evacuation (2), Crowd Control (2), Arrests/Search/Seizure (4).

**Not required to activate:** annual 8-hour BSIS refresher, many supplemental courses, weapons permits, medical, FEMA (optional extras may be verified later for client visibility).

### How activation works

1. **Moderator+** approves your **application** (`pending` → `approved`).
2. **Administrator+** verifies each of the five credentials in **Credentials**.
3. When all five are verified, your account **activates automatically** (`approved` → `active`).

If training/CE is still missing when activation rules allow a grace window, a **48-hour grace** may apply — upload before it expires or marketplace access can be restricted.

Official BSIS training reference: [bsis.ca.gov guard training](https://www.bsis.ca.gov/industries/g_train.shtml)

## 5. Set up pay

1. Open **Pay** → **Connect bank account** (Stripe Connect).
2. After jobs complete, payouts usually auto-release after the platform delay (~48 hours).
3. Use **Send to my bank** when earnings are available.

Payouts wait on client payment, the release schedule, and any open overtime/dispute holds.

---

## 6. Find work

### Marketplace job

1. Open **Map** → open a job pin/sheet.
2. Review location, schedule, pay, requirements, instructions.
3. **Slide to apply for job**.
4. Wait for the **client** to approve or decline.

### Direct request

1. Open the request on **Map**.
2. Review details → **Slide to claim job**.
3. Assignment appears under **Jobs**; shift controls appear when the window opens.

### Multi-guard jobs

Each guard applies for a roster slot independently. The client approves each guard. There is no crew coordinator role on Guardr.

---

## 7. Run a shift

Within about 24 hours of start, **Map** may show a **Next Job** card with countdown.

1. Review briefing → **Slide to start heading** (clients see live location from en route).
2. On site → **Slide to arrive on site** (GPS required).
3. **Slide to start job**.
4. Complete **self-audit**: selfie, uniform, shoes — or **Skip self audit** only when necessary (job flagged **No Self Audit** until you upload).
5. During the job:
   - **Message client**
   - **Report incident**
   - **Activity report**
6. End with **Slide to complete job** / **Slide to end shift** in the allowed window.
7. Optional client rating prompt; earnings show under **Pay** when rules are met.

Late completion can create overtime that you and the client must approve.

---

## 8. Overtime and help

1. **Jobs** → open the overtime-flagged job → **Approve overtime** if correct.
2. **Messages** for job chat; **Support** for tickets or **File a report**.

---

## 9. Performance, vehicle, driving

- **Performance**: Overall, Standing (foot/static), Driving (needs approved vehicle + valid insurance).
- **Vehicle**: Upload vehicle insurance under credentials first, save vehicle details, submit for staff approval. Expired insurance blocks driving access.

---

## 10. Typical sequence

```
Sign up → Upload creds → Application approved → Creds verified → Active
→ Connect bank → Apply/claim → Head / arrive / start + self-audit
→ Work → End shift → Payout
```

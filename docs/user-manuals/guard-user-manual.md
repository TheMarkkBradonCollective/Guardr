# Guardr Guard User Manual

For licensed, independent security professionals who find work on Guardr.

---

## Who you are on Guardr (read this first)

Guardr is operated by **Signature Security Specialist, LLC** as a **technology marketplace only**.

| You are | You are not |
|---------|-------------|
| An **independent contractor** who chooses which jobs to accept | An employee, agent, or partner of Guardr |
| In a **direct service relationship with the client** for each assignment | Dispatched, supervised, or employed by Guardr on site |
| Responsible for your own licenses, insurance, taxes, and business compliance | Covered by Guardr as your employer |

When you accept a job, you enter a **direct contract with that customer** for that assignment. Guardr provides the platform, payment facilitation, and credential review tools — **not** security supervision or employment.

On shift screens and job details, the hiring party is labeled **Customer** (personal or business account).

**Guardr staff** (Support through Founder) are **platform employees**. They verify marketplace eligibility and run operations. They do **not** employ you and do **not** assign normal field work — clients approve who works their site.

**Related:** [Quick Start](./quick-start.md) · [Client Manual](./client-user-manual.md) · In-app **Guide**

## 3. Credentials required to go live

Staff verify these before marketplace access. Nothing auto-verifies on upload.

| # | Credential | What to upload |
|---|------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Government ID** | Front, back, selfie, state, number, expiration |
| 2 | **Certificate of Insurance (COI)** | General liability COI |
| 3 | **BSIS Guard Card** | Registration card photo |
| 4 | **Mandatory training (PTA / UOF)** | Power to Arrest **and** Appropriate Use of Force as **separate** certificates |
| 5 | **Continuing Education** | Full **32-hour** first-year BSIS package (**9** certificates) |

**32-hour CE package (9 certificates):** Communication (4), Public Relations (4), Observation & Documentation (4), Liability/Legal (4), Officer Safety (4), Trespass (4), Evacuation (2), Crowd Control (2), Arrests/Search/Seizure (4).

**Not required to activate:** annual 8-hour BSIS refresher, many supplemental courses, weapons permits, medical, FEMA (optional extras may be verified later for client visibility).

### How activation works

1. **Moderator+** approves your **application** (`pending` → `approved`).
2. **Administrator+** verifies each of the five credentials in **Credentials**.
3. When all five are verified, your account **activates automatically** (`approved` → `active`).

Official BSIS training reference: [bsis.ca.gov guard training](https://www.bsis.ca.gov/industries/g_train.shtml)

## 5. How pay and payouts work (contractor earnings)

This section explains **your** money as an independent contractor. Guardr collects from the client and facilitates payment to you. This is **not** a wage or salary from Guardr.

### 5.1 What you see vs what the client pays

The client pays a **posted hourly rate** for the job. Guardr keeps a **platform fee** per hour from the client's **Personal** or **Business** fee table (and that guard type); you receive the remainder as **guard pay**. The fee on a posted job stays frozen even if Directors later change the live tables.

**Default example (platform settings may differ):**

| Item | Example |
|------|---------|
| Client hourly rate | $50.00 / hr |
| Platform fee (default flat) | $5.00 / hr |
| **Your guard pay** | **$45.00 / hr** |
| 8-hour shift | You earn **$360.00** before taxes/expenses |

On open-contract / negotiated jobs, the fee may be a flat dollar amount or a percentage of the client rate — the app shows **your rate and earnings** before you accept.

You do **not** see the client's full billing breakdown in guard views. Your **Pay** and job cards show **guard rate** and **guard earns**.

### 5.2 When earnings become available

Pay moves through stages after a shift:

```
Client pays → Job runs → Job complete → Pay released → You collect → Settled
```

| Stage | Meaning | Your action |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **Awaiting client payment** | Job not yet paid | None — wait for client |
| **Job active** | Paid and in progress | Work the shift |
| **Awaiting payout release** | Complete; pay not released yet | Wait (overtime/refunds may block) |
| **Ready to collect** | Pay released to you | Request a **bank transfer** on **Pay** |
| **Paid** | Funds sent or cash recorded | Done |

**Typical auto-release:** about **48 hours** after job completion if there are no holds.

**Payout can be blocked until:**

- Client payment is confirmed
- **Overtime** is approved and paid (or waived) by the client
- **Early clock-out refunds** are settled
- An active **dispute hold** is cleared (staff may release after resolution)

### 5.3 Stripe Connect (bank deposits)

1. Open **Pay** → **Connect bank account** (Stripe Connect Express).
2. Complete Stripe onboarding — bank details stay with Stripe, not stored in Guardr.
3. When earnings are **ready to collect**, use **Send to my bank** (or wait for auto-transfer if enabled).

Connect your bank through Stripe on **Pay**, then request a bank transfer once pay is released.

### 5.4 Requesting a payout

When multiple jobs are ready, **Pay** lets you submit a **payout request** (payout invoice). Staff fulfill bank transfers through the **Payments** pipeline. Track status on **Pay** until **Settled**.

### 5.5 Overtime and adjustments

If you clock out late, **overtime** may be calculated. You and the client must **approve** overtime before those hours pay out. Disputed overtime can hold your payout until staff resolve it.

### 5.6 Taxes and business responsibility

You are an **independent contractor**. Guardr does **not** withhold income tax, workers' compensation, or employment benefits. You are responsible for:

- Your own tax reporting and payments
- Maintaining valid BSIS registration and insurance
- Business expenses and compliance with client site rules

Accept the **Independent Contractor Agreement** and **Guard Code of Conduct** in-app before marketplace access.

## 6. Find work

### Marketplace job

1. Open **Map** → open a job pin/sheet.
2. Review location, schedule, **your pay**, requirements, instructions.
3. **Slide to apply for job**.
4. Wait for the **client** to approve or decline.

### Direct request

1. Open the request on **Map**.
2. Review details → **Slide to claim job**.
3. Assignment appears under **Jobs**; shift controls appear when the window opens.

### Multi-guard jobs

Each guard applies for a roster slot independently. The client approves each guard.

---

## 7. Run a shift

Within about 24 hours of start, **Map** may show a **Next Job** card with countdown.

1. Review briefing → **Slide to start heading** (clients see live location from en route).
2. On site → **Slide to arrive on site** (GPS required).
3. **Slide to start job**.
4. Complete **self-audit**: selfie, uniform, shoes — or **Skip self audit** only when necessary.
5. During the job: **Message client**, **Report incident**, **Activity report**.
6. End with **Slide to complete job** / **Slide to end shift** in the allowed window.
7. Optional client rating prompt; earnings show under **Pay** when rules are met.

**Timesheet:** **Profile → Timesheet** shows read-only clock audit (start/end times). You cannot edit shift times — the audit log is the record.

## 8. Overtime and help

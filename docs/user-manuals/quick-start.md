# Guardr Quick Start

**Anytime. Anywhere. Security, When You Need It.**

Pick the door that matches what you want. Guardr does **not** hire guards or clients through marketplace signup — platform jobs use **Apply to work at Guardr**.

Open [guardr.co](https://guardr.co) → create account or sign in.

---

## Path A — I need security (Client)

1. Choose **I need security for my site** and create a client account.
2. Finish your company profile (**Profile**).
3. Wait for staff approval (**Home** shows pending until approved).
4. **Post job offer** (or open **Guards** and send a direct request to one guard).
5. After staff approve the listing, **Pay** on the job when prompted.
6. For marketplace jobs: **Approve** or **Decline** guard applications on **Jobs**.
7. When the shift starts: confirm **self-audit photos**, use **Messages** / **Support** as needed.
8. After the shift: review reports, handle overtime if any, rate the guard.

Full steps: [Client User Manual](./client-user-manual.md)

---

## Path B — I'm a guard (Independent contractor)

1. Choose **I'm a guard** and create a guard account.
2. On the **activation screen**, upload the five required credentials while staff reviews your application:
   - Government ID
   - Certificate of Insurance (COI)
   - BSIS guard card
   - Power to Arrest + Appropriate Use of Force (separate certificates)
   - Continuing Education (32-hour BSIS package — 9 certificates)
3. Wait for **application approval**, then **credential verification**. Your account becomes **active** when all five required credentials are verified.
4. Open **Pay** → connect your bank (Stripe).
5. Open **Map** → apply to open jobs or **claim** a direct request.
6. On shift day: head to site → arrive → start job → self-audit photos → work → end shift.
7. Collect payouts from **Pay** after the job closes out.

Full steps: [Guard User Manual](./guard-user-manual.md)

---

## Path C — Apply to work at Guardr (Staff)

1. Choose **Apply to work at Guardr** (not guard or client signup).
2. Submit your staff application (experience, city, work history, availability).
3. Sign back in anytime to finish the **pending checklist**:
   - Application under Director review
   - Government ID (front, back, selfie)
   - Stripe bank connection for staff pay
4. When the checklist is complete, your account becomes **active** automatically.
5. Use the staff workspace for your role (Support, Moderator, Administrator, Manager, Director, or Founder).

Full steps: [Staff Ops Manual](./staff-ops-manual.md)

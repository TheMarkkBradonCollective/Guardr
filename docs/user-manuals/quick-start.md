# Guardr Quick Start

**Anytime. Anywhere. Security, When You Need It.**

Pick the door that matches what you want. Guardr runs **two separate worlds**:

| Marketplace | Guardr company (staff) |
|-------------|------------------------|
| **Clients** hire **independent contractor guards** | **Staff** are **employees** who operate the platform |
| Guards do **not** work for Guardr | Staff do **not** take guard shifts |
| Sign up: **I need security** → Personal or Business, or **I want to work** → licensed guard | Sign up: **I want to work** → **Apply to work at Guardr** |

Open [guardr.co](https://guardr.co) → create account or sign in. Returning marketplace hiring accounts use **Use the Customer app** on the role picker.

---

## Path A — I need security (Client)

You hire **licensed independent contractors** through the platform. They do **not** work for Guardr. Choose **Personal** (you pay) or **Business** (the company pays) — that is who contracts, not the type of location.

1. Choose **I need security**, then **Personal** or **Business**, and create a client account.
2. Finish your profile (**Profile**) and upload required **Credentials** (government-issued ID is always required).
3. Wait for staff approval (**Home** shows pending until approved).
4. **Request security** / **Post job** (or **Guards** → direct request / rebook).
5. After staff approve the listing, **Pay** on the job when prompted.
6. For marketplace jobs: **Approve** or **Decline** guard applications on **Jobs**.
7. During the shift: confirm **self-audit photos**, use **Messages** / **Support** as needed.
8. After the shift: review reports, handle overtime if any, rate the guard.

**What you pay:** Posted **hourly rate × hours × guards**, plus approved overtime, schedule extensions, and optional tips. Guardr retains a **platform fee** from that rate (separate Personal vs Business tables, per guard type); the guard receives the remainder. Posted jobs keep the fee saved at post time.

Full manual: [Client User Manual](./client-user-manual.md)

## Path B — I'm a guard (Independent contractor)

You are an **independent contractor** using Guardr to find clients. You do **not** work for Guardr.

1. Choose **I'm a licensed guard (contractor)** under **I want to work** and create a guard account.
2. On the **activation screen**, upload the five required credentials while staff reviews your application.
3. Wait for **application approval**, then **credential verification** → **active**.
4. Open **Pay** → connect your bank (Stripe Connect).
5. Open **Map** → apply to open jobs or **claim** a direct request.
6. On shift day: head → arrive → start → self-audit → work → end shift.
7. After pay is **released** (~48h typical), collect earnings on **Pay** (bank transfer via Stripe).

**What you earn:** **Guard pay** = client hourly rate minus platform fee (from the client's Personal or Business fee table for that guard type; often around **$5–$6/hr** unless negotiated). You contract **directly with the client** per job.

Full manual: [Guard User Manual](./guard-user-manual.md)

## Path C — Apply to work at Guardr (Staff employee)

You are applying for a **Guardr platform job** — not marketplace guard work.

1. Choose **Apply to work at Guardr** (under **I want to work** — not guard or client signup).
2. Submit your staff application (experience, city, work history, availability).
3. Sign back in to finish the **pending checklist**:
   - Application under **Director** review
   - Government ID (front, back, selfie)
   - **Stripe** bank connection for staff pay
4. When complete, your account becomes **active** automatically.
5. Use the staff workspace for your role (Support → Founder).

**How staff are paid:** **Revenue share** of collected platform fees (by role) + **Prop 22 hourly add-ons** from tracked timesheets + optional bonus. **No deductions.** Directors confirm payouts on **Payments**.

Full manual: [Staff Ops Manual](./staff-ops-manual.md)

# Guardr

**Anytime. Anywhere. Security, When You Need It.**

[guardr.co](https://guardr.co) is where businesses and property owners connect with **licensed security professionals** for on-demand coverage. Post a job, pick your guard, pay through the app, and track the shift live — or browse open work on the map and work on your own schedule.

Guardr is operated by **Signature Security Specialist, LLC**. We are a **technology marketplace** — not a security company, employer, or staffing agency. Guards and clients work together directly for each job.

## How it works

### For clients — posting a job

1. **Sign up** and complete your company profile.
2. Wait for **account approval** (usually quick — you'll see a banner on Home).
3. **Post a job** with the site address, schedule, pay rate, and any uniform or equipment requirements.
4. **Pay** for the job when prompted (card checkout in the app).
5. **Approve a guard** when someone applies — or send a **direct request** to a specific guard from the Guards page.
6. **Confirm self-audit photos** when the guard arrives and starts the shift.
7. **Review reports and rate** the guard after the shift ends.

You can also **request a specific guard** instead of posting to the open marketplace: open **Guards**, pick a profile, and send an assignment request.

### For guards — getting work

1. **Sign up** as a guard.
2. Upload your **required credentials** while your application is reviewed:
   - Government ID
   - California BSIS guard card
   - Certificate of insurance (COI)
   - Power to Arrest / Appropriate Use of Force training
   - 32-hour BSIS training block
3. Wait for staff to **verify your documents** and **activate your account**.
4. Connect your **bank account** in the Pay tab (Stripe).
5. Open the **Map**, browse open jobs, and **apply** — or **claim** a direct request from a client.
6. On shift day: **arrive on site** → **start shift** → complete your **self-audit photos**.
7. Work the shift, message the client, file reports if needed.
8. **End shift** when done — earnings appear in **Pay** after the job closes out.

Guards choose which jobs to take. Clients approve who works their site. Guardr does not assign or dispatch guards like a traditional security company.

## Payments

- **Clients** pay by card in the app when booking (Stripe checkout).
- **Guards** connect a bank account in **Pay** and receive earnings after the job is complete (typically within about 48 hours).
- **Overtime** from late clock-out may require approval from both guard and client before it is charged.

Guardr charges a platform fee on each job — built into the rate shown at booking. Guards and clients see pay details before confirming.

## Safety & accountability

- **Credential verification** — Guards must have verified ID, guard card, insurance, and training before they can work.
- **Self-audit at clock-in** — Guards submit selfie, uniform, and shoes photos at shift start; clients confirm them.
- **Incident reports** — Guards can file detailed incident reports during a shift; clients and staff can review them.
- **Activity reports** — Routine patrol notes logged during the shift.
- **Messaging** — Job chat between guard and client, plus support contact for help.
- **Ratings** — Clients rate guards after completed jobs.

## Important to know

**Guardr is a platform, not a security provider.**

- We do not employ guards, dispatch personnel, or perform security work on site.
- Guards are **independent contractors** — they choose their jobs, set availability, and are responsible for their own licensing, insurance, taxes, and on-site conduct.
- Clients and guards enter into a **direct service arrangement** for each job. Guardr provides the tools to find each other, book, pay, and document the work.
- Security licensing rules vary by state. Guardr is built with **California BSIS requirements** in mind; guards must meet credential requirements before going live on the marketplace.
